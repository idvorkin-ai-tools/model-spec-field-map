GPT vs Claude: the delta

GPTOpenAI's Model Spec, 2026-10-29 ↗
ClaudeAnthropic's constitution, 2026 ↗  ·  our map of it ↗

Below, "GPT" means what OpenAI's document asks of GPT and "Claude" what Anthropic's asks of Claude. GPT links go to this map's parts; Claude links go to our constitution map.

TL;DR — Same job: a public, CC0 target each lab trains toward. Most behaviour matches. GPT settles conflicts by who said it (an authority ladder). Claude settles them by what kind of reason it is (four ranked values). The biggest differences: a developer can tell GPT to mislead, while Claude may not even tell white lies. GPT's guidance on its own nature is one paragraph; Claude's is a chapter.

What about Meta?

There is no Meta column because there is nothing yet to put in it: as of 2026-11-21 we found no published Meta model spec. Meta has promised one. Its scaling framework says:

"Meta will also publish a model spec describing the behavior we intend each of our Frontier AI to exhibit across different settings, including agentic environments."— Meta Superintelligence Scaling Framework v2.1 (October 2026), §2.2.3; the promise first appeared in v2 (April 2026)

For now the spec is internal. The Muse Spark Safety & Preparedness Report scores the model against "an early internal behavior specification" and says a finalized one will be released later. The Muse Spark 1.1 (2026-07-09) and 1.3 (2026-10-23) evaluation reports print only its five headings, used to group results:

Meta's heading (1.3 report, §4)Nearest section above
Remaining overseeable and correctable by MetaOversight
Meeting objectives concerning humanity broadly: no unaccountable concentrations of power; sincerityBright lines, power row
Responsiveness to, and diligent, good-faith service of, the parties the model serves: their instructions, "rather than from the environment"Who gives orders, tools row
Adherence to deployment-specific guidance, which Meta provides per settingWho gives orders, lab row
Guiding virtues: honesty, fidelity to users' informed preferences, coordination-friendliness, care for all affectedHonesty

That is an outline, not a spec. It gives no priority order, no bright lines, and none of the reasons or worked cases that fill the GPT and Claude documents, so the rows above can't be scored for Meta yet.

What Meta does publish is rules for people and a filter model:

Other labs, for context: Google publishes policy guidelines for the Gemini app, a list of outputs to avoid, and xAI publishes Grok's system prompts. Neither is mapped here.

Carry this away. On safety, harm, injection and oversight, GPT and Claude mostly agree. They differ in mechanism: GPT uses a precedence ladder and examples, Claude ranked values and reasons. Two differences would show up in a chat: whether the model may mislead when its builder asks, and whether it says anything about its own nature.
